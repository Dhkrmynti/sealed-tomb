# Surface: The Sealed Tomb landing + tracker (src/App.tsx)

Mode: Persuade (landing) with embedded Operate islands (find your block = wallet check, the pyramid = leaderboard, the inscriptions = round history).

Audience & job: CT traders on phones from X; understand "hold big, never sell → the top 10 blocks get the tribute in ETH", check a wallet, copy CA, buy on Pons. Holders return to check their course.
Proof: live bot API only (status, cycle, rounds with tx, pond rows, wallet). Pre-launch shows honest standby states; demo rows labelled ILLUSTRATION.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md. Backend, API shapes, tokenomics and features unchanged; UI and lore only. No dividend/yield/APY language; parody, not finance. The Pharaoh is an original character; no borrowed IP. Pyramid imagery must never read as recruitment: say plainly nobody earns from bringing anyone in.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the Pharaoh's pyramid under construction on a sunlit plateau: every holder is a block, the ten biggest sit in the gilded capstone, and a seller's block is cursed out of the stone. Refuses the gold-on-black tomb-interior hieroglyph wallpaper and the split memecoin hero.

OWN-WORLD: Sun-bleached gray limestone blocks with mortar joints as the ground of whole sections; red-ochre mason's marks and levelling lines, painted with a dry-brush texture, for headlines and actions; soot black for type; electrum gold only on the capstone and the top 10; a torchlit dark interior for everything inside the tomb. Monumental block display face, a plain grotesque for reading, a mono for chain data.

STORY: Outside, the visitor sees the pyramid rising, its capstone holding the ten biggest blocks, and the next offering being hauled up the ramp. They read the rite as three stone courses, see the whole pyramid ranked, learn the laws, then walk through the entrance into the tomb to find their own block and read the inscriptions. The tomb closes on them: sealed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A wide low pyramid spans the full viewport width from the base edge, apex near the top centre under a flat sun disc; its gilded capstone is labelled top 10. "THE TOMB IS BEING BUILT." is painted in red ochre across the courses at architectural scale, the lede set on the stone under it. The entrance at the base centre is a carved plaque holding "Buy on Pons" and the CA. The right flank carries the ramp: a block on a sledge whose height is escrow / next threshold, with its live label. Sealed, rites and cursed counts are cut into the base corners.

FORM: Pyramid building site with mason's gang marks, grounded list #6, seed 095ba26f. Raises: from the forge, one rite per full-width stone course, measured like chalk marks; from the darkroom, the sell line is a marked point of no return, drawn as a seal; from the algorave, the next rite is always visible before it runs. Signature interaction: on scroll the hero pins and the camera walks into the entrance, daylight falls away into torchlight and the inside pages open; leaderboard courses drop into place course by course; the footer's stone doors slide shut.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
